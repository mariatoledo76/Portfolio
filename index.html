THE CHALLENGE

Job searching is hard.
Doing it in a new country is harder.

International students and recent graduates face unique challenges: unfamiliar job markets, visa restrictions, and a lack of clear guidance. Through interviews and research, we found that many feel overwhelmed, unsure where to start, and alone in the process.

Why it started

Built from the experience of international students and young professionals juggling applications, networking, school and life—and wanting support that felt more tailored than a job board.

Research → product

Customer interviews and empathy mapping informed the value proposition, MVP and testing. The product was simplified after users found too many features overwhelming.

What I contributed

01 / PILLSBURY · MARKETING METRICS & ANALYTICS

Convenient doesn’t always mean compelling.

Why was refrigerated cookie dough struggling to engage Canadian households?

The case analysis explored how consumers perceived Pillsbury against ready-to-eat cookies, competing dough brands and local bakeries. Convenience was only part of the choice: freshness, perceived quality and awareness also shaped the decision.

The approach

Usage-and-attitude findings, consumer segmentation and perceptual maps comparing convenience with perceived quality.

The question behind the chart

Do loyal customers, former customers and people who have never bought the product need the same message?

Three audiences. Three different barriers.

Heavy usersView perceptual map

Convenience is a strength. The opportunity is to build a stronger emotional connection and reinforce quality.

Lapsed usersView perceptual map

Freshness and quality perceptions matter. Re-engagement needs to address those barriers, rather than repeat convenience messaging.

Non-usersView perceptual map

Awareness and trial come first. Taste-led experiences can give people a reason to reconsider the product.

FROM INSIGHT TO RECOMMENDATION

Change the message with the audience.

Build loyalty through emotional storytelling for heavy users; address freshness and quality for lapsed users; use tastings and trial offers to reduce the first-purchase barrier for non-users.

These are recommendations from the academic case analysis, rather than reported campaign results.

02 / HULT SUPERSTORES · TABLEAU

Look beyond the sales total.

A dashboard for exploring where performance is strong—and where it needs a closer look.

The Tableau work brings sales, profit, sub-categories and regional performance together. Region filters and a focused view of underperforming categories help move from an overall picture to the part of the business that needs attention.

Sales ≠ profitability

Compare the sales mix with sub-category profit. A category can generate demand while still weakening the bottom line.

Context changes the answer

Use regional comparisons to investigate uneven performance instead of assuming the overall pattern applies everywhere.

What the analysis makes visible

The dashboard shows substantial differences in profit across sub-categories, including negative results in some categories. The workbook also includes sales-over-time views and calculations for profit margin and period-to-period sales growth.

THE BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Investigate before making the same decision everywhere.

Review category economics, discounting and regional differences together before recommending where to focus. The dashboard supports that investigation; it does not establish the cause of a profit gap on its own.
